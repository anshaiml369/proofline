Who has the pain

Three parties at every disputed handover

User

The rider

Hands over at doors, gates, receptions and security desks. When a claim goes against them, they have no way to show their side, and repeated claims can cost them the job.

Buyer

Platform dispute team

Pays refunds by default because it can't tell who is right. Wants evidence it can act on, scored the same way every time, with the final call still theirs.

Where

Every dark-store cluster in urban India

Quick-commerce and food delivery hand over orders in dense apartment blocks and offices, where "left with the guard" is a normal outcome a form can't record.

What we built

Hold one button, say one line, get a scored evidence card

Evidence card screenshot Rider page · 375px
(placeholder)

Five signals per handover

PhoneQR confirmationGPSPhotoSpoken context
  • Hinglish in, structure out. Sarvam codemix speech-to-text; the LLM only extracts fields: status, handover location, obstacle, instruction, confidence.
  • Fixed rules score it 0–100 and recommend favour rider, inconclusive or favour customer. The platform makes the final call.
  • Tamper-evident. SHA-256 of the audio plus server time. It proves the record wasn't edited later, not that the delivery happened.
  • Built for the doorstep. Works offline and uploads later; a dashboard and history for the dispute team; one API call for platforms.
