The problem

When a customer says “not delivered”, the platform cannot tell who is right

So it refunds the customer and lets the rider keep working. The platform absorbs the cost, and nobody learns what actually happened at the door.

How often. Public reporting and platform statements indicate that a significant share of contested delivery disputes default to refunding the customer while the rider continues working. UNVERIFIED

Why. The OTP proves a code was entered. It does not record how the handover went: who took the parcel, where, and what went wrong.

What is missing. No spoken record exists at the door. The rider's account is lost the moment they ride to the next order.

Who pays, and who buys. The platform's trust and safety team. It funds the refunds and decides the disputes, so it is the buyer.

The solution

Five signals, one evidence card, one rule-based score

Evidence card screenshot Rider page · 375px
(placeholder)
  • Phone: the customer's number, entered by the rider.
  • QR confirmation: the customer scans and taps “Yes, I received this”.
  • GPS: whether the rider was at the drop point.
  • Optional photo: stored with the record.
  • Spoken context: one line in Hindi, English or both, turned into fields: where, to whom, what went wrong.

Fixed rules score the card 0–100 and recommend favour rider, inconclusive or favour customer. The AI only transcribes and extracts; it never decides. The platform makes the final call.

The platform reviews evidence, not memories.

Why voice

At the door, speaking is the only input that fits

Hands on the parcel

The rider is holding the order, a bag and a phone. Hold one button and speak; no typing.

Faster than typing

Speaking one line is about 3× faster than typing it on a phone. UNVERIFIED

Says what a form can't

A form captures a checkbox. Voice captures the reason.

A form records “delivered ✓”. Voice records “gate locked, left at reception”: the sentence that decides the dispute.

Dependencies

Three outside services, each with a fallback

DependencyWhat it doesIf it gets costly or fails
Sarvam AISpeech-to-text in codemix mode: keeps Hinglish as spokenFalls back to OpenAI Whisper automatically
OpenRouterExtracts structured fields from the transcriptFalls back to OpenAI directly
Platform OTP / GPS APIsSends confirmation and location with each voice noteOur own QR confirmation and phone GPS work without them

If every AI service stops: the audio is already saved on our server, and its SHA-256 hash and the server's timestamp are computed before any AI call. Today the scored card still needs the transcript. Next step: store the hashed audio record even when transcription fails, so no handover is lost. TARGET

The AI layer is replaceable. The evidence (audio, hash, server time) is ours.
